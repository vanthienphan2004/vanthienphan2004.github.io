Human-Centered Healthcare: From Clinical Algorithms to Community Connection

At Viet Home Care LLC—an organization providing clinical and personal care for elderly patients across more than 1,000 households in Georgia—my work bridges two essential sides of eldercare: building machine learning and vision-language pipelines to streamline daily care operations, and spending time on the ground with seniors as a photographer for the Weekly Senior Community Center.

Data Science for Clinical & Home Care Operations

Artificial intelligence is advancing rapidly, yet clinicians, nurses, and care coordinators still end each day overburdened by routine administrative and documentation demands. Working directly within Viet Home Care's daily operations showed me how administrative backlogs and staffing constraints affect patient care across more than 1,000 households.

To support the care staff and improve operational efficiency, I focused on translating unstructured healthcare records and complex scheduling constraints into reliable, automated tools:

  • Vision-Language Pipelines for Clinical Documentation: Developed automated data pipelines integrating Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to extract and structure information from unstructured nursing notes, health coaching sessions, home health visit logs, and staff records—relieving nurses from manual data entry so they can dedicate more time to direct patient care.
  • Machine Learning for Caregiver–Patient Matching: Built classification and optimization models that pair elderly patients with the most suitable personal care aides based on clinical urgency, care needs, demographics, and compatibility across a pool of over 1,000 clients and aides.
  • Service Utilization & Operational Analytics: Analyzed utilization trends and workflow bottlenecks across home health programs, generating actionable insights that improved service delivery, process quality, and care outcomes for 1,000+ households.

Seeing firsthand how administrative backlogs and resource shortages directly impact vulnerable patients confirmed a core principle that guides my research: clinical algorithms must be built around real operational realities and human workflows rather than idealized assumptions.

Caring for both Health and Spirit: Weekly Senior Community Center

Supporting an aging population requires more than clinical care, medication schedules, and optimized caregiver queues—seniors need emotional warmth, social connection, and mental care just as much as clinical treatment. Loneliness and social isolation remain among the most pressing challenges for older adults, making community gathering spaces vital for their mental well-being and dignity.

Alongside my technical responsibilities as a Data Science Intern, I serve as a Photographer for the Weekly Senior Community Center at Viet Home Care. Each week—and during cultural festivals and holiday celebrations—I capture weekly activities, wellness gatherings, and joyful moments shared among the seniors and care team. Stepping out from behind the computer screen to talk, laugh, and document these memories grounds my technical work in the real human lives behind every healthcare dataset.
