Development of a Miniaturized Patient Lift System Using 3D Printing and Stepper Motor Control

Advisor Dr. Tin Tran
Presentation Engineering and Science Fair of Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (2022)
Award Consolation Prize at Engineering and Science Fair of HCMUT (2022)
Research Focus Biomedical Robotics & Assistive Devices
Hardware & Platform 3D Printing, Arduino Uno REV3 ICSP Microcontroller, Arduino A4988 Stepper Motor Driver

Abstract

Safe patient transfer in medical facilities and in-home care environments is critical for preventing fall-related injuries and reducing musculoskeletal strain among healthcare workers and caregivers. However, traditional mechanical and hydraulic patient lift systems are often excessively bulky, costly, and difficult to deploy in space-constrained settings.

In this project, we designed and built a functional, miniaturized patient lift machine using additive manufacturing (3D printing) combined with precision electromechanical actuation. The system is powered by an Arduino Uno REV3 ICSP microcontroller board paired with an Arduino A4988 stepper motor driver, enabling high-torque, stable vertical lifting and horizontal movement control.

Experimental testing demonstrated enhanced lifting and moving capabilities compared to conventional designs while simultaneously achieving a significant reduction in overall equipment size and weight. The project was presented at the Engineering and Science Fair of Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (2022) and recognized with the Consolation Prize.

Key Contributions

  • Developed a miniaturized prototype of an assistive patient lift machine using rapid 3D printing prototyping, Arduino A4988 Stepper Motor, and an Arduino Uno REV3 ICSP microcontroller board.
  • Enhanced mechanical lifting and moving capabilities as compared to existing models, while simultaneously reducing the device volume and footprint.
  • Awarded the Consolation Prize at the Engineering and Science Fair of Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (2022).
